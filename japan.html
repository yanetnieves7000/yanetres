Foods

  • Izakaya:Casual pubs serving small plates, yakitori (grilled skewers), and drinks.
  • Sushi/Omakase:Dedicated raw fish dining where chefs curate multi-course seasonal menus.
  • Ramen & Udon Shops:Fast-paced noodle shops where you often order via a vending machine ticket.
  • Kaiseki:Traditional multi-course Japanese dinners highlighting ultra-seasonal ingredients and artistic presentation.
  • Yakiniku & Shabu-Shabu: Interactive grill or hot-pot dining featuring Wagyu beef and sliced vegetables.
